# Threefold Passage
A puzzle expedition about complementary ways of moving obstacles.

## Language
**Hero**: A controllable member of the expedition with one fixed ability.
**Warden**: A hero who pushes an adjacent movable object one tile.
**Tether**: A hero who automatically drags the movable object directly behind when walking away.
**Seer**: A hero who must exchange places with the first movable object in the chosen cardinal direction whenever one is visible; otherwise the hero walks.
**Passage**: A bounded puzzle, solved when every hero stands on an exit.
**Crystal**: A movable obstacle shared by all three abilities.
**Exit**: A marked tile on which a hero must stand to complete a passage.
